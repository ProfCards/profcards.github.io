NEW CARD GAME RELEASED...

Vegetation Contention !!

This is our newest strategy card game fit for Age 12 and up. Play in a group of 2 to 5 players, or even in a party challenge game mode for as many players as possible!

Overview: Become the sole human to bring back peace in the unblemished nature by foraging & managing resources, crafting tools, evading & taming wildlife, maintaining personal health, and overcoming competitiors (players & environment) with various organic recipes!

OUR NEW TOOL WENT LIVE!

Card Game Playtester v.1

This free digital tool is accessible by all and very easy to use. With this, you can quickly test out your card game ideas!

It is also very useful as an educational resource. Ambitious students can train their creativity and critical thinking skills through the process of card game design, which can also involve research skills, reasoning skills, logical thinking skills, and much more.
